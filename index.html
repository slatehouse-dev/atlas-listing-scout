Atlas Listing Scout

Atlas Listing Scout is a private tool that one person runs on their own computer to keep track of alert emails. It is not a public service, nobody else can sign up for it, and it does not use advertising.

Privacy policy

What it accesses. With the owner's approval, it uses two Gmail permissions on the owner's own account: reading messages under one Gmail label, and sending alert emails to the owner.

What it does with that data. It reads details from those messages, compares them with the owner's saved criteria and records, and emails the owner about matches. Other mail is not used.

Where data is kept. Copies of those messages and the extracted details are stored on the owner's own computer until the owner deletes them.

Who else receives it. Details of a match are sent to Anthropic's Claude service to write a short summary, and alert emails pass through Google's Gmail service. Data is not sold, and is not shared with anyone else.

Removing access. The owner can remove this tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
